Privacy Policy for E-Launchpad

Last Updated: January 28, 2026

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
E-Launchpad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All profile information is read locally from your Microsoft Edge browser installation.

2. DATA STORAGE
- Theme preferences are stored locally on your device in %AppData%\E-Launchpad\settings.json
- No data is sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking is implemented

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
This application does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

4. EDGE PROFILE DATA
E-Launchpad reads Edge profile information (profile names and icons) directly from your local Edge installation. This data is:
- Never transmitted outside your device
- Never stored by our application (read-only access)
- Used solely to display profile selection options

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
E-Launchpad does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

7. CONTACT
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official channels.
