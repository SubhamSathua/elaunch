1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
E-Launchpad does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The Application operates entirely offline and locally on your device. We do not:
- Collect personal information
- Collect usage statistics or analytics
- Track user behavior
- Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
- Send any data to external servers
- Integrate with advertising networks

2. LOCAL DATA ACCESS
E-Launchpad accesses the following data stored locally on your device:

A. MICROSOFT EDGE PROFILE DATA (Read-Only)
   - Profile names
   - Profile icons/avatars
   - Profile folder locations

   This data is:
   - Read directly from your local Microsoft Edge installation
   - NEVER transmitted outside your device
   - NEVER stored or cached by E-Launchpad
   - Used solely to display profile selection options within the Application

B. APPLICATION SETTINGS
   - Location: %AppData%\E-Launchpad\settings.json
   - Contains: Theme preferences and window settings
   - This file is stored locally on your device only
   - You may delete this file at any time without affecting your system

3. DATA STORAGE
All data used by E-Launchpad remains on your local device:

- No cloud storage is used
- No external servers are contacted
- No network connections are required for the Application to function
- No data synchronization occurs

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
E-Launchpad does NOT integrate with any third-party services, APIs, SDKs, or
analytics platforms.

The Application is completely self-contained and does not communicate with any
external services.

5. DATA SHARING
We do NOT share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties because
we do not collect any data.

6. DATA SECURITY
Since E-Launchpad does not collect or transmit data:
- There is no risk of data breach from our servers (we have none)
- All information remains under your control on your local device
- Your Microsoft Edge profile data is accessed read-only

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
E-Launchpad does not collect any information from any users, including children
under 13 years of age. Since no data is collected, stored, or transmitted:
- No parental consent mechanisms are required
- No age verification is performed or needed
- The Application's privacy-preserving design is consistent with children's
  privacy protection principles

8. USER RIGHTS
Since no personal data is collected, stored, or processed by E-Launchpad:

- There is no personal data to access, correct, or delete
- To remove all Application data, simply uninstall E-Launchpad and delete the
  settings folder at %AppData%\E-Launchpad

Under GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations, you retain all rights to your
data, which remains entirely on your device.
